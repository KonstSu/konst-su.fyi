Profile

Detector electronics engineer with more than 10 years of experience in instrumentation for free-electron laser and accelerator facilities, with hands-on work on X-ray detectors and sensors (DEPFET, LGAD). I take detector and instrument electronics from requirements through design, integration and commissioning to safe operation.

Work package leader for electronics development in detector development projects at European XFEL. Appointed VEFK for the detector laboratories. Executive PhD candidate in Engineering Physics and Materials at Ca’ Foscari University of Venice, working on test instrumentation and AI-assisted model-based systems engineering for X-ray detectors.

Experience

2023 – present
Engineer for Detector Electronics
European XFEL, Germany · Detectors Group
  • Project leadership: work package leader for electronics development in detector development projects.
  • Test systems: led the design, assembly and commissioning of a test setup for single readout chips and new X-ray sensors, including power control, temperature regulation, Karabo integration and DAQ.
  • Detector integration: commissioned the AGIPD 4M cabling, power-supply interlock and motion system at the SPB/SFX instrument. Supported DSSC installation and operation at SCS.
  • Calibration instrumentation: developed the JUNGFRAU backplane pulser, moving it from an ESP32 to a Zynq SoC platform, and a GOTTHARD-II calibration device.
  • Electronics development: took fast X-ray detector electronics from concept to working unit: schematics, PCB layout, prototyping and documentation.
  • ASIC design: since 2025, architecture design of an analogue front-end for a pixel readout ASIC.
  • Methods and process: introduced MBSE with SysML v2, an AI-assisted engineering workflow and a structured PCB development process to the group’s electronics projects. Coach colleagues in AI-assisted engineering.
  • Electrical safety: VEFK and ESP for the detector laboratories. Responsible for DGUV V3 inspections and for procedures for high-voltage and high-current setups.
2016 – 2023

  • Managed the full lifecycle of instrument electronics, from requirements definition to integration and operation.

  • Ran trade-off analyses (function, cost, quality, schedule) for the evaluation and procurement of critical instrument components.

Glossary

AGIPD
Adaptive Gain Integrating Pixel Detector, a megapixel X-ray detector at European XFEL
DSSC
DEPFET Sensor with Signal Compression, a soft X-ray imaging detector
DEPFET
Depleted p-channel field-effect transistor, a pixel sensor with built-in amplification
LGAD
Low-gain avalanche diode
JUNGFRAU, GOTTHARD-II
X-ray detectors developed at the Paul Scherrer Institute
DAQ
Data acquisition
ASIC
Application-specific integrated circuit, a custom chip; here, the readout chip of a pixel detector
JINST, JACoW
Journal of Instrumentation; Joint Accelerator Conferences Website, the publisher of accelerator conference proceedings
Karabo
The control and data acquisition software framework of European XFEL
HED, MID, SPB/SFX, SCS
European XFEL scientific instruments: High Energy Density; Materials Imaging and Dynamics; Single Particles, Clusters and Biomolecules & Serial Femtosecond Crystallography; Spectroscopy and Coherent Scattering
MBSE, SysML v2
Model-based systems engineering; Systems Modeling Language, version 2
VEFK
Verantwortliche Elektrofachkraft: the person responsible for electrical safety in an organisational unit under German rules
EFK
Elektrofachkraft: electrically skilled person
DGUV V3
German accident-prevention regulation for electrical installations and equipment, including their periodic inspection
