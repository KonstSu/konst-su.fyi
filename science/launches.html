Launches

Upcoming launches to the planets and small bodies, with a short list for the Moon. Dates are “no earlier than”.

The 2026 Mars window (November to December)

  • MMX (JAXA) launched on 19 October 2026 on an H3-24L: mission page.
  • ESCAPADE (NASA) launched back in November 2025 and uses this window through its Earth flybys on 7 and 9 November: mission page.
  • Starship (SpaceX): in 2025 Elon Musk put the chance of an uncrewed flight in the 2026 window at 50/50 (Al Jazeera). A Mars Starship is not in the launch schedules, and SpaceX appears to have shifted its focus to the Moon. There has been no official cancellation; it is assumed here that it will not fly in 2026.

No other interplanetary launches are scheduled before the end of 2026.
