Hera

Orbiter with two CubeSats (Milani and Juventas)

Status
Arriving
Launched
7 Oct 2024
Target
Binary asteroid (65803) Didymos and its moon Dimorphos
Agencies
ESA
Next
25 Nov 2026 ESA expects to release the first detailed images of Dimorphos

Summary

ESA's planetary-defence mission, a "crash-scene investigation" at Dimorphos, the asteroid moonlet that NASA's DART spacecraft hit in 2022, changing its orbit. Hera will measure its mass, structure and crater so that the kinetic-impactor technique can be modelled precisely.

Now as of 3 Nov 2026

The spacecraft is on its final approach. After braking manoeuvres in October its speed relative to the asteroids is about 20 m/s. On 18 October the AFC camera resolved Didymos and Dimorphos as two separate objects for the first time, from 1,280 km away (ESA). According to heise, arrival was due on 4 November, and ESA expects to publish the first detailed images of Dimorphos on 25 November. The approach is stepwise: 25–30 km, then 10, 4 and 1 km; the CubeSats will be released "in the coming months".

Sizes: Didymos is 780 m across, Dimorphos 151 m; on average they are 1.15 km apart.
