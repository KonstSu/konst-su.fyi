Summary

A mission to asteroid (16) Psyche, possibly the exposed metal core of a protoplanet. The spacecraft flies on Hall-effect thrusters; it also carried the DSOC deep-space laser-communication demonstrator.

Now as of 3 Nov 2026

On 15 May 2026 it made a Mars gravity assist at about 4,609 km. NASA released images in batches (the Martian crescent, Syrtis Major, Fourier crater, the Oenotria Scopuli scarps) and a time-lapse covering 2–15 May (JPL, Sky at Night). It is now cruising on electric propulsion towards the asteroid belt.
