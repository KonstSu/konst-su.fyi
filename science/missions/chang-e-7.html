Mission · Moon

Chang'e-7

Orbiter, lander, rover and a small hopping probe

Status
Awaiting launch
Launch
2027 (planned)
Target
The Moon, south pole, rim of Shackleton crater
Agencies
CNSA
Next
2027 launch on a Long March 5; CNSA has not given a new date

Summary

China's expedition to the south pole of the Moon: an orbiter, a lander, a rover and a small hopping probe that will look for water ice in a shadowed crater (Wikipedia).

Now as of 13 Nov 2026

The launch did not take place: the spacecraft missed the window of 23–24 August 2026 (Space.com). Instrument partners speak of 2027; no official new date or explanation from CNSA has been found (Wikipedia).
