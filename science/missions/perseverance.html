Summary

NASA's Mars rover, searching for signs of ancient life and collecting samples for a future return to Earth. It has been working since February 2021.

Now as of 3 Nov 2026

On 11 October 2026 it set the record for distance driven on another world: the odometer read 45.22 km, passing Opportunity (45.16 km). The record day was a 91.31 m drive (NASA blog, Planetary Society). Its radioisotope power source is working normally and should last many more years (DD India / Reuters). The future of the Mars Sample Return programme remains uncertain.
