Lucy

Flyby spacecraft with a record number of targets

Summary

The first mission to Jupiter's Trojan asteroids, "fossils" from the era when the planets formed. Over 12 years it will fly past more than eight asteroids.

Now as of 3 Nov 2026

Cruising towards the Trojans at the L4 point. The first Trojan is Eurybates (about 64–68 km, C-type) with its moon Queta, in August 2027. NASA gives both 11 and 27 August, trackers give 12 August (SwRI — mission tour).
