OSIRIS-APEX

Orbiter (formerly OSIRIS-REx)

Status
Status unclear
Launched
8 Sep 2016
Target
Asteroid (99942) Apophis
Agencies
NASA, University of Arizona
Next
13 Apr 2029 Apophis passes close to Earth; the spacecraft arrives afterwards

Summary

OSIRIS-REx, which delivered samples of Bennu in 2023, has been redirected to Apophis. On 13 April 2029 this 340-metre asteroid will pass about 32,000 km from Earth, and the spacecraft will study how tidal forces have shaken it up.

Now as of 3 Nov 2026

Cruising. The last Earth gravity assist was on 23 September 2025, at 3,438 km (NASA SVS). Funding is at risk: the President's budget request for fiscal year 2026 proposed ending the mission, and I have not found Congress's final decision for it in the available sources.
