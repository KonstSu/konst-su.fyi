Summary

PROMISE (Polar Rover for Observation, Mapping, and In-Situ Exploration) is a six-wheeled lunar rover with a nuclear power source, able to work through the lunar night and in permanently shadowed craters. JPL is building it from flight spares and engineering models of the Curiosity and Perseverance Mars rovers. Its instruments: a drill reaching about 1 m deep, spectrometers, temperature and radiation sensors, cameras and the High Performance Spaceflight Computing on-board computer (NASA, 7 November 2026).

Now as of 13 Nov 2026

Announced on 7 November 2026 at the MAX POWER conference at Kennedy Space Center. Delivery was awarded to Blue Origin on a Blue Moon Mark 1 lander: a base contract of $94 million plus an option worth $140 million. Launch is planned for late 2028, and the rover is designed to work for 18 months (NASA).
