Summary

The orbiter of the ExoMars programme: it searches the Martian atmosphere for trace gases such as methane, maps water ice and relays data from the rovers. It launched on 14 March 2016, arrived at Mars on 19 October 2016 and has been in its science orbit since April 2018 (ESA — TGO, EPSC 2026, C. Wilson). It began as a joint mission of ESA and Roscosmos.

Now as of 9 Nov 2026

Operating. According to ESA, the spacecraft is in nominal condition and could work "well into the 2030s" (EPSC 2026, C. Wilson). Together with Mars Express it probes the ionosphere by radio occultation: more than 300 vertical profiles have been obtained since 2020 (EPSC 2026, J. Parrott). The sources found do not say how long the current funding runs.
