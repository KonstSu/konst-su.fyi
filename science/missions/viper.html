VIPER (Volatiles Investigating Polar Exploration Rover)

Rover

Summary

NASA's lunar rover that will search for water ice near the south pole, with a 1 m drill and spectrometers. The project was cancelled in July 2024; on 19 September 2025 NASA announced that Blue Origin would deliver the rover on a Blue Moon Mark 1 lander (CLPS task order CS-7) (NASA).

Now as of 13 Nov 2026

The rover is fully assembled and is in final testing at Johnson Space Center in Houston, after which it goes to the launch site; the flight is in 2027, with no exact date (NASA, 20 October 2026). This is not the first flight of Blue Moon Mark 1, "Endurance" (Moon Base I): that one carries other NASA payloads and has not launched yet. In May NASA gave "no earlier than autumn 2026" for it (NASA, 26 May 2026); Wikipedia, citing the journalist Jeff Foust, gives "no earlier than the first quarter of 2027" (Wikipedia).
