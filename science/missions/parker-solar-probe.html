Parker Solar Probe

Solar probe

Status
Extended mission
Launched

Target
The Sun (corona)
Agencies

Next

Summary

The spacecraft that "touches" the Sun: since 24 December 2024 it has passed 6.1 million km from the surface at about 690,000 km/h, records for closeness and speed for any human-made object.

Now as of 3 Nov 2026

After the 2026 Heliophysics Senior Review the mission was extended to 2029 (reported by Wikipedia, citing NASA). Perihelia in 2026: number 27 on 11 March, 28 on 8 June, 29 on 4 September (UCAR WHPI). The next, number 30, should come around early December based on the 88-day orbit; that is an estimate, not an official date.
