Summary

The first interplanetary mission of an Arab country: the UAE's orbiter studies the atmosphere and weather of Mars across the whole planet. It has been in Mars orbit since 2021; the UAE became the fifth country to reach Mars (Khaleej Times, 17 February 2026). It launched on 19 July 2020 (UTC) and entered orbit on 9 February 2021 (Wikipedia).

Now as of 9 Nov 2026

On 17 February 2026 the mission was extended until 2028. The spacecraft was designed for one Martian year and has worked for five Earth years, and a technical assessment confirmed that it is in excellent condition. The extended mission will concentrate on the link between the lower and upper atmosphere of Mars. The extension was announced by the chairman of the UAE Space Agency (Khaleej Times).
