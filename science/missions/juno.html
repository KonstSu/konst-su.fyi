Summary

In orbit around Jupiter since 2016, studying its interior, magnetic field and aurorae; the extended mission added flybys of Ganymede, Europa and Io.

Now as of 3 Nov 2026

The status is unclear. The last officially announced extension ended on 30 September 2025, in the middle of a US government shutdown, and NASA did not confirm at the time whether the spacecraft was still operating (Space.com). The budget request for fiscal year 2027 again proposes to end Juno (Planetary Society). Papers based on Juno data keep appearing: in October 2026, one on Jupiter's "shield" of plasma waves and shock regions against the solar wind (ScienceDaily) and one on its rippling magnetodisk (Universe Today). One source claims an extension to September 2028; I found no official confirmation.
