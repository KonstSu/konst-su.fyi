Summary

The first rover on the far side of the Moon: it landed with the Chang'e-4 lander in Von Kármán crater in January 2019 and became the longest-lived lunar rover (The Planetary Society).

Now as of 13 Nov 2026

Uncertain. According to the Planetary Society, the Chang'e-4 lander continues its science work, while the rover has apparently not moved since March 2024, and it is unclear whether it still works (The Planetary Society). No recent official CNSA reports have been found. Chinese media gave a distance driven of about 1.6 km as of September 2024 (not checked against a primary source).
