Summary

One of NASA's two STEREO spacecraft, which observe the Sun from different points along Earth's orbit so that plasma eruptions can be seen in three dimensions. They launched on 26 October 2006. The second spacecraft, STEREO-B, has been silent since 2014, and its mission was closed on 17 October 2018 (NASA — STEREO). In August 2023 STEREO-A passed Earth for the first time in the mission: NASA planned its crossing of the Sun–Earth line for 12 August (NASA Goddard), and it took images at a higher rate during the pass (STEREO news).

Now as of 9 Nov 2026

Operating normally, according to the mission's weekly minutes; the latest found are from 22 September 2026 (STEREO, 22 September 2026). In August 2026 some contacts were lost because of a wildfire near the Deep Space Network station in Madrid (STEREO, 18 August 2026).
