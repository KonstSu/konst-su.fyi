Summary

A European probe that uses Venus flybys to tilt its orbit gradually out of the ecliptic plane, so that it can see the Sun's poles for the first time.

Now as of 3 Nov 2026

A Venus flyby in February 2025 raised the orbit's inclination to 17°, and the first-ever images of a solar pole followed. The next Venus flyby, on 24 December 2026, raises the inclination to 24° and starts the high-latitude phase; later flybys take it to 33° (ESA, perihelia and flybys; ESA notes that the dates may shift).
