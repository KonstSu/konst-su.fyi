Summary

NASA's rover studying whether the environment in Gale crater was once habitable. It launched on 26 November 2011 and landed on 6 August 2012; since 2014 it has been climbing the slope of Mount Sharp, which is about 5 km high (NASA — Curiosity, NASA, 26 August 2026).

Now as of 9 Nov 2026

On 26 August 2026 the rover had gained 1 km in elevation on its way from the crater floor up Mount Sharp, which NASA calls the highest point of the 14-year mission (NASA). On 29 August 2026 it passed 5,000 sols; by September 2026 it had driven about 37 km (EarthSky, 24 September 2026).

In April 2026 NASA reported the most diverse set of organic molecules found on Mars so far: 21 carbon-containing compounds in a rock sampled in 2020, seven of them seen on Mars for the first time (NASA, 21 April 2026). In summer 2026 the rover was climbing towards a suspected erosion surface (a "discontinuity") in the sedimentary layers (mission blog, 11 August 2026).
