Hayabusa2# (extended mission)

Extended mission after returning samples from Ryugu in 2020

Status

Jul 2031 rendezvous with 1998 KY26

Summary

The spacecraft that delivered samples of asteroid Ryugu in 2020 is still flying: it passed asteroid Torifune (2001 CC21) and will rendezvous with the tiny, fast-spinning 1998 KY26 in 2031.

Now as of 3 Nov 2026

On 5 July 2026 it flew past Torifune at about 5 km/s. The ONC-T camera showed an elongated two-lobed asteroid shaped like a snowman; the TIR thermal imager observed it too (Sci.News). Secondary reports put the flyby distance at about 800 m from the centre; I found no JAXA confirmation of that figure. Brian May (yes, that one) and Claudia Manzoni turned the images into a stereo pair (brianmay.com).
