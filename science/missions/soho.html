Summary

A joint solar observatory of ESA and NASA at the L1 point. It was launched on 2 December 1995 for a two-year mission and has worked for more than 30 years; in March 2024 it discovered its 5,000th comet, more than any other observatory in history (ScienceDaily / ESA, 3 December 2025).

Now as of 9 Nov 2026

Uncertain. Under a 2023 decision, ESA's contribution to SOHO was confirmed until the end of 2025, after which the mission was expected to wind down, with a two-year post-operations phase from 1 January 2026 (ESA, 7 March 2023). In December 2025 the Finnish Meteorological Institute, a participant in the mission, wrote that ESA and NASA had extended SOHO "at least into 2026" (FMI, 5 December 2025). No official decision with a new end date has been found. The SOHO website is still being updated in 2026 (SOHO).
