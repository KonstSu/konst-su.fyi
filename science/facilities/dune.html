Facility · Neutrinos

DUNE / LBNF

Liquid-argon time projection chambers and a neutrino beam from Fermilab to the Sanford Lab (1,300 km)

Status
Under construction
Location
Fermilab (Illinois) to SURF (South Dakota), USA
Operator
DUNE Collaboration, Fermilab (US Department of Energy)
Next
≈ 2030 data taking begins (after delays)

Summary

A long-baseline experiment for CP violation, the neutrino mass ordering and supernova neutrinos. The far detector consists of modules of about 17 kt of liquid argon each, about 1.5 km underground.

Now as of 4 Nov 2026

Underground work is under way: the first steel cryostat is being lowered into the mine. Scientific American reported in May 2026 that delays have added about five years to the schedule, with data taking now expected around 2030 and the total cost approaching 5 billion dollars (summary by Pulse).
