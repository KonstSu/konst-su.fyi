Vera C. Rubin Observatory (LSST)

8.4 m survey telescope with a 3.2-gigapixel camera

Status
Operating
Location
Cerro Pachón, Chile
Operator
NSF NOIRLab and DOE SLAC
Next
Data previews and first LSST science releases

Summary

The ten-year Legacy Survey of Space and Time: the whole southern sky every few nights in six filters. It has the world's largest digital camera (built at SLAC) and sends millions of alerts per night.

Now as of 4 Nov 2026

The LSST survey officially began on the night of 29 June 2026 (Princeton LSST, Fermilab). The first public alerts went out on 24 February 2026 (SEA).
