SHINE (Shanghai High repetitIon rate XFEL aNd Extreme light facility)

Superconducting high-repetition-rate hard X-ray FEL (8 GeV, up to 1 MHz)

Status
Commissioning
Location
Zhangjiang, Shanghai, China
Operator
ShanghaiTech University, SARI and SIOM (Chinese Academy of Sciences)
Next
2027 (end) completion and start of use (planned)

Summary

A superconducting X-ray free-electron laser under construction since April 2018, the third continuous-wave superconducting XFEL after European XFEL and LCLS-II. The design: an 8 GeV linac, up to 1 MHz, and first-phase undulator lines covering 0.2–15 keV with femtosecond pulses.

Now as of 23 Nov 2026

First lasing has been achieved. On 21 October 2026 Xinhua, citing ShanghaiTech University, reported that SHINE had "successfully achieved free-electron laser first lasing", "a key step towards completion and use by the end of 2027". The linac currently runs as a 4 GeV continuous-wave superconducting machine (8 GeV after completion), with 18 undulators of 4 m and a 55 mm period (Xinhua). The report gives neither the exact date, nor the undulator line, nor the photon energy. The commissioning plan presented at IPAC'26 aimed at first lasing around 200 eV on the FEL-II line at about 4 GeV (JACoW IPAC'26), so the soft X-ray line is the likely candidate; this is not confirmed.
