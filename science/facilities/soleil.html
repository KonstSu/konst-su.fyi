SOLEIL / SOLEIL II

2.75 GeV storage ring, to become SOLEIL II (emittance from 3,900 to 90 pm·rad)

Status
Operating
Location
Saint-Aubin (Paris-Saclay plateau), France
Operator
Synchrotron SOLEIL (CNRS, CEA)
Next
Oct 2028 SOLEIL shuts down; SOLEIL II "dark period" (about two years including commissioning)

Summary

France's 2.75 GeV synchrotron. SOLEIL II brings a new ultra-low-emittance ring and a new booster at the same energy: natural emittance from 3,900 to 90 pm·rad (L. Nadolski, ESLS 2025); it extends the facility's life by at least 15 years (SOLEIL II).

Now as of 9 Nov 2026

Preparing for the shutdown. The RF roadmap: conditioning from August to December 2026; the first two 352 MHz cavities installed in the present ring in July–August 2027; the first 1.4 GHz harmonic cavity in January 2028; SOLEIL shuts down in October 2028 (ESLS 2025). The dark period starts in October 2028 and lasts about two years including commissioning (DEELS 2026, N. Hubert). Users would return around the end of 2030; this is an estimate from those dates, and no official date has been found.
