Facility · Neutrinos

Hyper-Kamiokande

Water Cherenkov detector (~260 kt) with a neutrino beam from J-PARC

Status
Under construction
Location
Kamioka, Japan
Operator
Hyper-K Collaboration, ICRR and KEK
Next
2027 photomultiplier installation; 2028 — first data (plan)

Summary

The successor to Super-Kamiokande, about eight times larger in volume. Its goals are CP violation in neutrinos (with the J-PARC beam), proton decay and neutrinos from supernovae. A direct competitor of DUNE.

Now as of 4 Nov 2026

Excavation of the cavern was completed in summer 2025; the tank is being built in 2026, photomultipliers will be installed in 2027, and first data are expected in 2028 (PoS, Hyper-K status, CERN Indico, January 2026).
