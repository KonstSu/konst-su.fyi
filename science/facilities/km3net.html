Facility · Neutrinos

KM3NeT (ARCA and ORCA)

Deep-sea Cherenkov neutrino telescopes

Status
Under construction
Location
Mediterranean Sea, off Sicily (ARCA) and off Toulon (ORCA)
Operator
KM3NeT Collaboration
Next
ARCA and ORCA construction continues; new analyses of KM3-230213A

Summary

Two detectors under construction: ARCA (astrophysics, high energies) and ORCA (oscillations, neutrino mass ordering). They take data as they grow; the partly built ARCA has already recorded a record event.

Now as of 4 Nov 2026

The main topic is the event KM3-230213A (13 February 2023, estimated energy ~220 PeV), the most energetic neutrino ever recorded, published in Nature in February 2025. In 2026 the collaboration showed in JCAP that a population of blazars could explain the event without conflicting with gamma-ray and neutrino limits; other papers discuss a possible Galactic origin (KM3NeT/ANTARES, JCAP, PDF). No second ultra-high-energy event has been reported so far.
