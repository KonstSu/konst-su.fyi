LHC / HL-LHC

13.6 TeV proton collider; high-luminosity upgrade

Status
Long shutdown
Location
CERN, Geneva (Switzerland and France)
Operator
CERN
Next
Jun 2030 HL-LHC starts (Run 4)

Summary

The largest collider. The High-Luminosity LHC will increase the integrated luminosity about tenfold, with new Nb₃Sn quadrupoles, crab cavities and upgraded ATLAS and CMS detectors.

Now as of 4 Nov 2026

Run 3 is over, and Long Shutdown 3 began at the LHC on 29 June 2026. The LHC stops for 47 months; the injectors ran until September 2026. HL-LHC (Run 4) is due to start in June 2030 (CERN, CERN ATS News).
