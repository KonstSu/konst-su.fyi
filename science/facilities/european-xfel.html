European XFEL

Where I work

Superconducting X-ray free-electron laser, up to 27,000 pulses per second

Status
Operating
Location
Schenefeld and Hamburg, Germany
Operator
European XFEL GmbH (12 member countries); accelerator operated by DESY
Next
User operation; facility upgrade in preparation

Summary

The world's largest X-ray free-electron laser: a 1.7 km superconducting linear accelerator at 17.5 GeV, trains of up to 2,700 pulses at an intra-train rate of 4.5 MHz, three SASE beamlines and seven instruments. This megahertz pulse structure is what made dedicated detectors necessary (AGIPD, LPD, DSSC, JUNGFRAU).

Now as of 4 Nov 2026

Back in user operation after its first long shutdown with a warm-up of the accelerator (LIMP, 2025). The restart was celebrated on 11 February 2026, and the user programme resumed in spring 2026 (idw). A new photoinjector, GUN5, was installed during the shutdown (DESY, lightsources.org). Preparations for a facility upgrade are under way; the public upgrade page describes the options being studied.
