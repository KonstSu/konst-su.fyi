Results

Notable scientific results from large facilities and space missions: what was found, with the paper (DOI or arXiv) and the press release where there is one.

November 2026

  1. 20 Nov 2026X-ray lasers30 keV (≈0.04 nm) X-ray laser pulses delivered reliably over the ~1 km beamline at European XFEL: 800 pulses per second with almost 60 % transmission; the facility's previous record (2020) was 25 keV lasing. Aimed at experiments on dense matter and at extreme pressures — Optica (DOI not yet indexed) — lightsources.org; facility page: European XFEL
  2. 12 Nov 2026Gravitational wavesLVK: long-transient searches around merger remnants from O4b and O4c disfavour ultralight vector bosons with masses of ≈2.80–3.95 × 10⁻¹³ eV (above 90 % confidence) — Phys. Rev. D 114, 103025, 10.1103/dgjv-chmt
  3. 5 Nov 2026X-ray lasersA simulation study: a plasma-photocathode wakefield accelerator could drive a high-gain X-ray laser in the water window (mJ, GW-class pulses) from a plasma stage about a metre long; not an experiment — Berman L. et al., Nature Communications, 10.1038/s41467-026-78880-4 — idw
  4. 2 Nov 2026Particle physicsALPS II at DESY in Hamburg, a "light shining through a wall" experiment, first science run (February–May 2024): no signal; for pseudoscalar particles below ~0.1 meV the two-photon coupling is limited to g < 1.5 × 10⁻⁹ GeV⁻¹ (95 % CL), more than 20 times better than earlier experiments of this type. This is still far from the design goal of 2 × 10⁻¹¹ GeV⁻¹, and the optics are being upgraded — Brotherton D. et al., PRL, 10.1103/61sw-k3bc; preprint arXiv:2512.14110

October 2026

  1. 30 Oct 2026DetectorsAn iLGAD sensor from PSI detects soft X-rays down to the nitrogen K-edge — preprint arXiv:2610.42064
  2. 29 Oct 2026SynchrotronsAltermagnetism confirmed in MnTe: a SLAC-led team saw chiral magnons with circularly polarised X-rays on ESRF beamline ID32 — Jost D. et al., Chiral altermagnon in MnTe, Science 394, 541–543, 10.1126/science.adv3196 — SLAC; facility page: ESRF
  3. 29 Oct 2026Space scienceJuno: Jupiter protects itself from the solar wind with a system of plasma waves and several shock regions (paper in Nature Communications, 31 July) — ScienceDaily; mission page: Juno
  4. 21 Oct 2026X-ray lasersSHINE: first lasing of China's superconducting high-repetition-rate X-ray laser (accelerator at 4 GeV for now); undulator line and photon energy not given — Xinhua; facility page: SHINE
  5. 20 Oct 2026X-ray lasersAttosecond hard X-ray pulses at LCLS: 100–400 as at ~9 keV, the shortest high-energy pulses yet from an X-ray laser — Inoue I. et al., Nature Photonics 20, 1303–1308, 10.1038/s41566-026-02026-7 — SLAC; facility page: LCLS
  6. 8 Oct 2026X-ray lasersThree-dimensional imaging of single membrane-protein complexes in vacuum with an X-ray laser — preprint arXiv:2610.11729
  7. 6 Oct 2026NeutrinosNobel Prize in Physics for Francis Halzen, for IceCube and the discovery of high-energy astrophysical neutrinos — Nobel Prize; facility page: IceCube

July to September 2026

  1. 23 Sep 2026X-ray lasersRecord intensity for attosecond hard X-ray pulses at European XFEL: ~300 as pulses focused to 180 × 330 nm with AKB mirrors, above 10²⁰ W/cm² — Inoue I. et al., Optica, 10.1364/optica.613284 (press release on 16 October: idw)
  2. 22 Sep 2026Gravitational wavesLVK: a direct search for several kinds of ultralight dark matter (scalar, dark photon, tensor) in O4a data finds no signal, with limits orders of magnitude better than earlier experiments — Phys. Rev. Lett. 137, 131001, 10.1103/qdnd-rsnh — LIGO summary
  3. 7 Aug 2026Laser physicsCorrelated electrons push high-harmonic generation in the X-ray range beyond the single-electron limit — Wang S. et al., Nature Photonics 20, 1092–1098, 10.1038/s41566-026-01976-2 (press release in October: ScienceDaily)
  4. 22 Jul 2026Space scienceJuno: first measurements of the temperature below Io's surface — Mission Juno
  5. 5 Jul 2026Space scienceHayabusa2: flyby of asteroid Torifune, which turns out to have two lobes — Sci.News; mission page: Hayabusa2#

January to June 2026

  1. 29 Jun 2026TelescopesThe Rubin Observatory begins its ten-year LSST survey — Princeton LSST
  2. 10 Jun 2026NeutrinosJUNO: first physics result (Nature, cover), Measurement of reactor neutrino oscillation with the first JUNO data: with 59 days of data, the precision on two oscillation parameters improves by about a third — IHEP, China Daily
  3. 26 May 2026Gravitational wavesGWTC-5.0: the second part of O4 (data to 28 January 2025); more than 300 merging black-hole and neutron-star binaries in total — preprint arXiv:2605.27223
  4. 22 May 2026Particle physicsThe CERN Council updates the European Strategy: FCC-ee is the preferred next flagship collider; construction is not approved, a decision is aimed for 2028, and the first phase is estimated at about €16 billion — FCC, heise
  5. 2026NeutrinosKM3NeT: a blazar interpretation of the ~220 PeV neutrino (JCAP) — PDF
  6. 28 Jan 2026X-ray lasersLasing of a cavity-based X-ray source (XFELO) at European XFEL: Rauer P. et al., Lasing of a cavity-based X-ray source, Nature 650, 93–96 — 10.1038/s41586-025-10025-x, CFEL
  7. Jan 2026NeutrinosIceCube Upgrade: deployment completed — UW News
