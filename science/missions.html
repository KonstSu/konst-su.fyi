Missions

Spacecraft beyond Earth orbit, from the Sun to interstellar space. Status, next milestone and key dates for each, with links to the agencies.

Destination
Status
  • SunExtended missionParker Solar ProbeNASA, JHU APLThe spacecraft that "touches" the Sun, closer and faster than anything humans have built.

    Next ≈ Dec 2026 perihelion 30 (estimated from the 88-day orbit)

  • SunStatus unclearSOHOESA, NASAThe ESA–NASA solar observatory launched in 1995 for two years; after more than 30 years its status for 2026 is not officially confirmed.

    Next Status for 2026 not officially confirmed; reported as extended "at least into 2026

  • SunAt targetSolar OrbiterESA, NASAUses Venus flybys to tilt its orbit and look at the Sun's poles.

    Next 24 Dec 2026 Venus flyby raises the orbit's tilt to 24°

  • SunExtended missionSTEREO-ANASANASA's solar observatory that watches the Sun and its plasma eruptions from the side, away from the Sun–Earth line.

    Next No key dates; observations of the Sun and its eruptions continue

  • MercuryArrivingBepiColomboESA, JAXATwo orbiters, one European and one Japanese, about to enter orbit around Mercury.

    Next 21 Nov 2026 Mercury orbit insertion

  • Earth orbitAt targetProject SuncatcherGoogle, PlanetGoogle's research project on machine-learning hardware in orbit; in October 2026 its prototype returned the first model response from space.

    Next 2027 a pair of satellites linked by laser

  • MoonEndedArtemis IINASA, CSAThe first crewed flight to the Moon since Apollo 17; Orion flew around the Moon and returned in April 2026.

    Next Mission completed on 10 April 2026

  • MoonAwaiting launchArtemis IIINASA, ESAReshaped in 2026 from a Moon landing into a crewed docking test with two lunar landers in Earth orbit, planned for mid-2027.

    Next 2027 launch; mid-year according to NASA

  • MoonAwaiting launchArtemis IVNASAAfter the programme was reshaped, this is the flight that should put people back on the Moon, in 2028.

    Next 2028 launch and first landing; NASA's mission page says early 2028

  • MoonAwaiting launchChang'e-7CNSAChina's expedition to the lunar south pole in search of water ice; it missed its August 2026 launch window.

    Next 2027 launch on a Long March 5; CNSA has not given a new date

  • MoonAwaiting launchFLIPAstrolab, Astrobotic, NASAAstrolab's half-tonne rover on Astrobotic's Griffin-1 lander, a test bed for a future crewed Moon rover.

    Next ≈ Jan 2027 launch of Griffin-1 on a Falcon Heavy; NASA had said late 2026

  • MoonAwaiting launchLTV: CLV-1 and PegasusNASA, Astrolab, Lunar Outpost, Blue OriginTwo open rovers for the Artemis astronauts, ordered by NASA in 2026 and due on the Moon by 2028.

    Next 2027 flight vehicles handed over to NASA at the end of the year; delivery to the Moon by 2028

  • MoonAwaiting launchPROMISENASA, JPL, Blue OriginA nuclear-powered lunar rover built by JPL from spare Mars-rover parts, announced in November 2026 for launch in late 2028.

    Next 2028 launch at the end of the year on Blue Moon Mark 1

  • MoonAwaiting launchVIPERNASANASA's ice-hunting lunar rover, cancelled in 2024 and revived in 2025; it is to fly on Blue Origin's Blue Moon Mark 1 in 2027.

    Next 2027 launch on a Blue Moon Mark 1 lander

  • MoonStatus unclearYutu-2CNSAThe first rover on the far side of the Moon and the longest-lived lunar rover; it has apparently not moved since March 2024.

    Next Status not confirmed; the rover has not moved since March 2024

  • MarsExtended missionCuriosityNASA, JPLNASA's rover has been climbing Mount Sharp since 2014; in August 2026 it passed 1 km of elevation gain and 5,000 sols.

    Next No key dates; the climb up Mount Sharp continues

  • MarsCruiseESCAPADENASA, UC Berkeley SSL, Rocket LabTwin small spacecraft that will watch the solar wind strip the Martian atmosphere from two points at once.

    Next 7–9 Nov 2026 Earth flyby and burn towards Mars

  • MarsExtended missionExoMars TGOESA, Roscosmos (formerly)ESA's orbiter that searches the Martian atmosphere for trace gases and relays data from the rovers.

    Next No key dates; atmospheric observations and data relay continue

  • MarsExtended missionHopeUAE Space Agency, MBRSCThe first interplanetary mission of an Arab country, studying Martian weather; extended in 2026 until 2028.

    Next 2028 end of the extended mission

  • MarsExtended missionMars ExpressESAESA's first mission to Mars, in orbit since 2003; funded until the end of 2026, with an extension to 2029 requested.

    Next 31 Dec 2026 current funding ends; an extension to the end of 2029 has been requested

  • MarsExtended missionMars OdysseyNASA, JPLThe oldest working spacecraft at Mars, 25 years in orbit, and now running low on fuel.

    Next Fuel is running low; the mission is expected to end within the next few years (estimate)

  • MarsLostMAVENNASA, GSFC, LASPStudied how Mars lost its atmosphere; contact was lost in December 2025 and the mission has ended.
  • MarsCruiseMMXJAXA, CNES, DLR, NASA, ESAJapan's mission to land on Phobos and bring the first samples from the Mars system back to Earth.

    Next 2027 arrival at Mars, about a year after launch

  • MarsExtended missionMRONASA, JPLNASA's orbiter with the HiRISE camera; since the loss of MAVEN, one of the main relays for the Mars rovers.

    Next No key dates; imaging and relay of rover data continue

  • MarsExtended missionPerseveranceNASA, JPLNASA's rover searching for signs of ancient life; it now holds the record for distance driven on another world.

    Next No key dates; work continues on the rim of Jezero crater

  • MarsExtended missionTianwen-1CNSAChina's first Mars mission; the orbiter appears to be working, though CNSA has published nothing on it in 2026.

    Next No key dates; operations in Mars orbit

  • Asteroids & cometsExtended missionHayabusa2#JAXAThe spacecraft that brought back Ryugu samples, now on its way to a tiny, fast-spinning asteroid.

    Next Jul 2031 rendezvous with 1998 KY26

  • Asteroids & cometsArrivingHeraESAESA's planetary-defence mission, arriving at the asteroid that NASA's DART knocked off course.

    Next 25 Nov 2026 ESA expects to release the first detailed images of Dimorphos

  • Asteroids & cometsCruiseLucyNASA, SwRIThe first mission to Jupiter's Trojan asteroids, fossils from the time the planets formed.

    Next Aug 2027 flyby of Trojan (3548) Eurybates and its moon Queta

  • Asteroids & cometsStatus unclearOSIRIS-APEXNASA, University of ArizonaThe spacecraft that returned samples of Bennu, redirected to Apophis for its close pass by Earth in 2029.

    Next 13 Apr 2029 Apophis passes close to Earth; the spacecraft arrives afterwards

  • Asteroids & cometsCruisePsycheNASA, JPL, ASUOn its way to an asteroid that may be the exposed metal core of a protoplanet.

    Next Aug 2029 arrival at the asteroid (plan)

  • Asteroids & cometsAt targetTianwen-2CNSAChina's first asteroid mission: samples from a small quasi-moon of Earth, then on to a comet.

    Next 2026–2027 site selection and sampling; departure ≈ April 2027

  • JupiterCruiseEuropa ClipperNASA, JPLNASA's largest planetary spacecraft, going to find out whether Europa's ocean could support life.

    Next 3 Dec 2026 Earth gravity assist (~3,200 km)

  • JupiterCruiseJuiceESAESA's flagship to Jupiter's icy moons, which will become the first spacecraft to orbit another planet's moon.

    Next Jan 2029 final Earth gravity assist

    Next No confirmed events; mission status unclear

    Next 2028–2029 — leaves the Kuiper belt; search for a new flyby target continues

    Next 18 Nov 2026 Voyager 1 one light-day from Earth
