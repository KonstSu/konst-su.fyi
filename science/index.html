  1. 20 Nov 2026X-ray lasers30 keV (≈0.04 nm) X-ray laser pulses delivered reliably over the ~1 km beamline at European XFEL: 800 pulses per second with almost 60 % transmission; the facility's previous record (2020) was 25 keV lasing. Aimed at experiments on dense matter and at extreme pressures — Optica (DOI not yet indexed) — lightsources.org; facility page: European XFEL
  2. 12 Nov 2026Gravitational wavesLVK: long-transient searches around merger remnants from O4b and O4c disfavour ultralight vector bosons with masses of ≈2.80–3.95 × 10⁻¹³ eV (above 90 % confidence) — Phys. Rev. D 114, 103025, 10.1103/dgjv-chmt
  3. 5 Nov 2026X-ray lasersA simulation study: a plasma-photocathode wakefield accelerator could drive a high-gain X-ray laser in the water window (mJ, GW-class pulses) from a plasma stage about a metre long; not an experiment — Berman L. et al., Nature Communications, 10.1038/s41467-026-78880-4 — idw
  4. 2 Nov 2026Particle physicsALPS II at DESY in Hamburg, a "light shining through a wall" experiment, first science run (February–May 2024): no signal; for pseudoscalar particles below ~0.1 meV the two-photon coupling is limited to g < 1.5 × 10⁻⁹ GeV⁻¹ (95 % CL), more than 20 times better than earlier experiments of this type. This is still far from the design goal of 2 × 10⁻¹¹ GeV⁻¹, and the optics are being upgraded — Brotherton D. et al., PRL, 10.1103/61sw-k3bc; preprint arXiv:2512.14110
  5. 30 Oct 2026DetectorsAn iLGAD sensor from PSI detects soft X-rays down to the nitrogen K-edge — preprint arXiv:2610.42064
