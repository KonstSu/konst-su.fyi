## Summary
The most distant human-made objects, both now in interstellar space. As the power of their radioisotope generators declines, instruments are being switched off one by one; most science instruments are already off, but both spacecraft are still in contact.

## Now
On **18 November 2026** Voyager 1 becomes the first spacecraft one light-day from Earth (about 25.9 billion km): its signal will take 24 hours to reach us. Science News gives the time as 05:16:07 EST (10:16 UTC); other reports mentioned 13 and 15 November, which were earlier estimates ([Science News](https://sciencenews.org/article/voyager-1-light-day-exploration), [EarthSky](https://earthsky.org/space/voyager-1-1-light-day-from-earth-november-17-18-2026/)). The yearly swing in distance caused by Earth's own orbit will no longer bring it back inside that mark.
