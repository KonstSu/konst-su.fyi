## Summary
Flew past Pluto (2015) and the Kuiper belt object Arrokoth (2019). It is the only working spacecraft in the Kuiper belt.

## Now
The extended mission runs until the spacecraft leaves the Kuiper belt, expected in 2028–2029. The search for a new flyby target goes on: in June 2026 the team reported 294 new Kuiper belt objects from a survey with the Subaru Telescope ([Subaru Users Meeting 2026, PDF](https://subarutelescope.org/Science/SubaruUM/Talks/O67_Verbiscer_subaru_um2026.pdf)). Funding was debated for a long time ([Universe Today](https://www.universetoday.com/articles/new-horizons-is-funded-through-the-decade-enough-to-explore-another-kuiper-belt-object)).
