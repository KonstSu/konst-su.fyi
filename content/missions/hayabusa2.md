## Summary
The spacecraft that delivered samples of asteroid Ryugu in 2020 is still flying: it passed asteroid Torifune (2001 CC21) and will rendezvous with the tiny, fast-spinning 1998 KY26 in 2031.

## Now
On 5 July 2026 it flew past Torifune at about 5 km/s. The ONC-T camera showed an elongated two-lobed asteroid shaped like a snowman; the TIR thermal imager observed it too ([Sci.News](https://www.sci.news/space/hayabusa-2-close-up-images-asteroid-torifune-14902.html)). Secondary reports put the flyby distance at about 800 m from the centre; I found no JAXA confirmation of that figure. Brian May (yes, that one) and Claudia Manzoni turned the images into a stereo pair ([brianmay.com](https://brianmay.com/brians-soapbox/2026/07/torifune-the-newest-japanese-asteroid-encounter/)).
