## Summary
NASA's Mars rover, searching for signs of ancient life and collecting samples for a future return to Earth. It has been working since February 2021.

## Now
On **11 October 2026** it set the record for distance driven on another world: the odometer read 45.22 km, passing Opportunity (45.16 km). The record day was a 91.31 m drive ([NASA blog](https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/perseverance-mars-rover/2026/10), [Planetary Society](https://www.planetary.org/the-downlink/a-drive-to-remember)). Its radioisotope power source is working normally and should last many more years ([DD India / Reuters](https://ddindia.co.in/2026/10/nasas-perseverance-rover-breaks-record-for-off-planet-driving-on-mars/)). The future of the Mars Sample Return programme remains uncertain.
