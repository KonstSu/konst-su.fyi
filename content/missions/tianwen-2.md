## Summary
China's first asteroid mission: collect samples from Kamoʻoalewa, a small quasi-satellite of Earth (possibly a fragment of the Moon), return the capsule to Earth, and then fly on to the main-belt comet 311P/PanSTARRS.

## Now
The spacecraft is at the asteroid, imaging it and choosing a sampling site. The asteroid spins once in about 28 minutes, which makes touching down harder. Two sampling methods will be tried: touch-and-go and anchor-and-attach. At the time of checking there were no official reports of sampling.
