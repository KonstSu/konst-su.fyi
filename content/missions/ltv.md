## Summary
Unpressurised rovers for the Artemis astronauts that can also drive without a crew. In May 2026 NASA placed two fixed-price orders: $219 million to Astrolab for CLV-1 (based on FLEX, about 900 kg, faster than 9.7 km/h) and $220 million to Lunar Outpost for Pegasus (a development of the Eagle rover, faster than 14.5 km/h). Blue Origin is to deliver both to the south pole for $188 million plus an option worth $280.4 million ([NASA, 26 May 2026](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-provides-update-on-moon-base-rovers-landers-missions/)).

## Now
Both companies are on schedule and are due to hand over the flight vehicles to NASA at the end of 2027; Blue Origin takes them to the Moon "by 2028". The preliminary design review (PDR) was scheduled for October 2026 at Lunar Outpost and by the end of December 2026 at Astrolab ([NASA, 20 October 2026](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/moon-base/nasa-provides-progress-update-on-moon-base-rovers/)). Whether the Lunar Outpost review took place has not yet been confirmed. NASA does not say which Artemis mission the rovers are meant for. On 7 November 2026 the same Blue Origin order was extended to include delivery of the [PROMISE](mission:promise) rover ([NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-awards-moon-base-transportation-technology-science-projects/)).
