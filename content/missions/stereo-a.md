## Summary
One of NASA's two STEREO spacecraft, which observe the Sun from different points along Earth's orbit so that plasma eruptions can be seen in three dimensions. They launched on 26 October 2006. The second spacecraft, STEREO-B, has been silent since 2014, and its mission was closed on 17 October 2018 ([NASA — STEREO](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/stereo/)). In August 2023 STEREO-A passed Earth for the first time in the mission: NASA planned its crossing of the Sun–Earth line for 12 August ([NASA Goddard](https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/goddard/after-seventeen-years-a-spacecraft-makes-its-first-visit-home/)), and it took images at a higher rate during the pass ([STEREO news](https://stereo-ssc.nascom.nasa.gov/news/news.xml)).
