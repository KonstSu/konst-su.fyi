## Summary
NASA's lunar rover that will search for water ice near the south pole, with a 1 m drill and spectrometers. The project was cancelled in July 2024; on 19 September 2025 NASA announced that Blue Origin would deliver the rover on a Blue Moon Mark 1 lander (CLPS task order CS-7) ([NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/viper/)).

## Now
The rover is fully assembled and is in final testing at Johnson Space Center in Houston, after which it goes to the launch site; the flight is in 2027, with no exact date ([NASA, 20 October 2026](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/moon-base/nasa-provides-progress-update-on-moon-base-rovers/)). This is not the first flight of Blue Moon Mark 1, "Endurance" (Moon Base I): that one carries other NASA payloads and has not launched yet. In May NASA gave "no earlier than autumn 2026" for it ([NASA, 26 May 2026](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-provides-update-on-moon-base-rovers-landers-missions/)); Wikipedia, citing the journalist Jeff Foust, gives "no earlier than the first quarter of 2027" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Moon_Pathfinder_Mission_1)).
