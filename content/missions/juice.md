## Summary
ESA's flagship mission to Jupiter's icy moons. It will study the subsurface oceans of Ganymede, Callisto and Europa, and in 2034 it will become the first spacecraft to orbit the moon of another planet (Ganymede).

## Now
On 28 September 2026 it passed over the Indian Ocean at an altitude of 8,640 km. The Earth gravity assist went as planned and needed only one small correction out of six scheduled. The instruments operated from 23 September to 3 October and imaged the Earth and the Moon ([ESA](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Successful_Earth_flyby_improves_Juice_s_course_to_Jupiter), [ESA video](https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Videos/2026/10/Juice_s_September_2026_Earth_flyby_the_movie)).
