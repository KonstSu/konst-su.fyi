## Summary
The first rover on the far side of the Moon: it landed with the Chang'e-4 lander in Von Kármán crater in January 2019 and became the longest-lived lunar rover ([The Planetary Society](https://www.planetary.org/space-missions/change-4)).

## Now
Uncertain. According to the Planetary Society, the Chang'e-4 lander continues its science work, while the rover has apparently not moved since March 2024, and it is unclear whether it still works ([The Planetary Society](https://www.planetary.org/space-missions/change-4)). No recent official CNSA reports have been found. Chinese media gave a distance driven of about 1.6 km as of September 2024 (not checked against a primary source).
