## Summary
A mission to asteroid (16) Psyche, possibly the exposed metal core of a protoplanet. The spacecraft flies on Hall-effect thrusters; it also carried the DSOC deep-space laser-communication demonstrator.

## Now
On 15 May 2026 it made a Mars gravity assist at about 4,609 km. NASA released images in batches (the Martian crescent, Syrtis Major, Fourier crater, the Oenotria Scopuli scarps) and a time-lapse covering 2–15 May ([JPL](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia26749-nasas-psyche-mission-images-details-of-martian-surface-during-flyby/), [Sky at Night](https://www.skyatnightmagazine.com/news/nasa-psyche-mars-flyby-15-may-2026)). It is now cruising on electric propulsion towards the asteroid belt.
