## Summary
China's expedition to the south pole of the Moon: an orbiter, a lander, a rover and a small hopping probe that will look for water ice in a shadowed crater ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chang'e_7)).

## Now
The launch did not take place: the spacecraft missed the window of 23–24 August 2026 ([Space.com](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/bad-weather-delays-launch-of-chinas-historic-change-7-mission-to-moons-south-pole)). Instrument partners speak of 2027; no official new date or explanation from CNSA has been found ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chang'e_7)).
