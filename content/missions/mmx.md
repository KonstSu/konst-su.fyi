## Summary
A Japanese mission to orbit Phobos, land, collect samples and bring them back to Earth. If it succeeds, these will be the first samples from the Mars system. It carries the French–German IDEFIX rover and instruments from NASA and ESA.

## Now
Launched on 19 October 2026 at 19:41 UTC (20 October, 04:41 Japan time) on an H3-24L rocket (flight F10) from Tanegashima ([Space.com](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/japan-launches-historic-mmx-sample-return-mission-to-mars-moon-phobos), [nippon.com](https://www.nippon.com/en/news/yjj2026102000118/)). The spacecraft is on its way to Mars. It is the only interplanetary launch of the 2026 Mars window.
