## Summary
Studied how Mars lost its atmosphere and relayed data from the rovers.

## Now
**The mission has ended.** Contact was lost on 6 December 2025 after the spacecraft passed behind Mars; it had entered safe mode and was spinning fast. After solar conjunction (until 16 January 2026) contact could not be restored, and in early June 2026 NASA declared the mission over ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MAVEN), [AIAA](https://aiaa.org/2026/03/23/nasa-continues-efforts-to-reestablish-contact-with-maven-at-mars/)). As a result, NASA's remaining orbiters at Mars are MRO and Mars Odyssey; ESA's ExoMars TGO also relays data.
