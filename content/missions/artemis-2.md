## Summary
The first crewed flight to the Moon since Apollo 17: the Orion spacecraft, launched on the SLS rocket, flew around the Moon and returned to Earth. The crew were Reid Wiseman, Victor Glover and Christina Koch (NASA) and Jeremy Hansen (CSA) ([NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/artemis-ii/)).

## Now
The mission is complete. It launched on 1 April 2026 and splashed down in the Pacific off San Diego on 10 April 2026 after 9 days, 1 hour and 32 minutes ([NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/artemis-ii/)). According to press reports, splashdown was at 17:07 PDT (00:07 UTC on 11 April), and for the first time the crew were taken out of the capsule in open water ([Caltech Tech](https://tech.caltech.edu/2026/04/21/artemis-ii-returns-crew-to-earth-demonstrates-new-recovery-operations-for-lunar-missions/)). The flight was a test of Orion ahead of the next missions ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/04/11/nx-s1-5776712/after-a-whirlwind-mission-to-the-moon-astronauts-are-back-home-heres-whats-next)).
