## Summary
The first mission to Jupiter's Trojan asteroids, "fossils" from the era when the planets formed. Over 12 years it will fly past more than eight asteroids.

## Now
Cruising towards the Trojans at the L4 point. The first Trojan is Eurybates (about 64–68 km, C-type) with its moon Queta, in August 2027. NASA gives both 11 and 27 August, trackers give 12 August ([SwRI — mission tour](https://lucy.swri.edu/mission/Tour.html)).
