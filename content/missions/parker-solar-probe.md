## Summary
The spacecraft that "touches" the Sun: since 24 December 2024 it has passed 6.1 million km from the surface at about 690,000 km/h, records for closeness and speed for any human-made object.

## Now
After the 2026 Heliophysics Senior Review the mission was extended to 2029 (reported by [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parker_Solar_Probe), citing NASA). Perihelia in 2026: number 27 on 11 March, 28 on 8 June, 29 on 4 September ([UCAR WHPI](https://whpi.hao.ucar.edu/whpi_campaign-psp29p.php)). The next, number 30, should come around early December based on the 88-day orbit; that is an estimate, not an official date.
