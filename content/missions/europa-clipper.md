## Summary
NASA's largest planetary spacecraft. Its goal is to find out whether the ocean beneath Europa's icy crust could be habitable: the thickness of the ice, its composition, possible plumes of water. About 49 close flybys of Europa are planned.

## Now
Cruising. On **3 December 2026** it makes an Earth gravity assist at an altitude of about 3,200 km; together with the Mars flyby of March 2025 this gives it the energy to reach Jupiter ([NASA, mission timeline](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/europa-clipper/mission-timeline)). The flyby is the only chance after launch for an absolute calibration of the magnetometer against Earth's magnetic field ([EGU26](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU26/EGU26-7780.html)). Dates of 1 and 4 December also circulate; they are older estimates or a different time zone.
