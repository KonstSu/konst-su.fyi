## Summary
OSIRIS-REx, which delivered samples of Bennu in 2023, has been redirected to Apophis. On 13 April 2029 this 340-metre asteroid will pass about 32,000 km from Earth, and the spacecraft will study how tidal forces have shaken it up.

## Now
Cruising. The last Earth gravity assist was on 23 September 2025, at 3,438 km ([NASA SVS](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/15101)). Funding is at risk: the President's budget request for fiscal year 2026 proposed ending the mission, and I have not found Congress's final decision for it in the available sources.
