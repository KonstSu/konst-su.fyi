## Summary
A European probe that uses Venus flybys to tilt its orbit gradually out of the ecliptic plane, so that it can see the Sun's poles for the first time.

## Now
A Venus flyby in February 2025 raised the orbit's inclination to 17°, and the first-ever images of a solar pole followed. The next Venus flyby, on **24 December 2026**, raises the inclination to 24° and starts the high-latitude phase; later flybys take it to 33° ([ESA, perihelia and flybys](https://esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Solar_Orbiter/Solar_Orbiter_perihelia_and_flybys); ESA notes that the dates may shift).
