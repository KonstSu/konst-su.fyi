## Summary
A joint ESA–JAXA mission: two orbiters, Europe's MPO (mapping, interior, surface) and Japan's Mio (magnetosphere), are flying to Mercury as one stack. It will be only the second mission ever to orbit Mercury, after NASA's MESSENGER.

## Now
The Mercury Transfer Module (MTM) separated on 3 September 2026; since then MPO has been propelling the MPO + Mio stack. MPO's instruments have been checked and are in good health. On **21 November 2026** Mercury's gravity captures the spacecraft and it enters orbit; MPO and Mio separate on 9–10 December. The science phase begins in April 2027 ([ESA, live updates](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/BepiColombo/Latest_updates_BepiColombo_s_arrival_at_Mercury)).
