## Summary
A crewed test ahead of the landing. In 2026 the mission was reshaped: instead of landing on the Moon, Orion will stay in low Earth orbit and practise rendezvous and docking with prototype landers from Blue Origin (Blue Moon Mark 2) and SpaceX (Starship HLS) ([NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/artemis-iii/), [NASA release, 9 June 2026](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-marches-toward-artemis-iii-mission-in-2027-names-crew-members/)). The crew was named on 9 June 2026: commander Randy Bresnik, pilot Luca Parmitano (ESA), mission specialists Andre Douglas and Frank Rubio; the backup is Bob Hines ([NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-marches-toward-artemis-iii-mission-in-2027-names-crew-members/)).

## Now
In preparation for launch. The sources word the date differently: the mission page says "2027" ([NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/artemis-iii/)), a briefing on 23 October 2026 said "mid-2027" ([SpacePolicyOnline](https://spacepolicyonline.com/news/nasa-is-full-steam-ahead-on-artemis-iii-iv-and-v/)), and local press reported "summer 2027" ([ClickOrlando](https://www.clickorlando.com/news/local/2026/10/23/artemis-iii-on-schedule-for-summer-2027-launch/)). In September NASA's administrator called the schedule tight; the bottlenecks are the readiness of the landers and the spacesuits ([SpacePolicyOnline](https://spacepolicyonline.com/news/nasa-is-full-steam-ahead-on-artemis-iii-iv-and-v/)).

The flight plan given at the briefing: a Blue Moon Mark 2 test article launches first and waits in orbit for up to 30 days; Orion flies on an SLS with a spacer in place of the ICPS upper stage, docks on day 3 and spends about two days with the lander; Starship launches on day 6, and the docking with it lasts about two hours, without the crew going inside; the whole mission takes about 14 days ([SpacePolicyOnline](https://spacepolicyonline.com/news/nasa-is-full-steam-ahead-on-artemis-iii-iv-and-v/)). NASA's release describes the Starship docking differently, as lasting "about a day" ([NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-marches-toward-artemis-iii-mission-in-2027-names-crew-members/)). The mission page lists a news item of 28 October 2026 on joining the SLS core stage to its boosters ([NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/artemis-iii/)).
