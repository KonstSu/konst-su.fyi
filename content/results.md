## November 2026
- **2026-11-20 · xfel** — **30 keV (≈0.04 nm) X-ray laser pulses delivered reliably over the ~1 km beamline** at European XFEL: 800 pulses per second with almost 60 % transmission; the facility's previous record (2020) was 25 keV lasing. Aimed at experiments on dense matter and at extreme pressures — Optica (DOI not yet indexed) — [lightsources.org](https://lightsources.org/2026/11/20/european-xfel-takes-x-ray-laser-to-extremely-short-wavelengths/); facility page: [European XFEL](facility:european-xfel)
- **2026-11-12 · gw** — LVK: long-transient searches around merger remnants from O4b and O4c disfavour ultralight vector bosons with masses of ≈2.80–3.95 × 10⁻¹³ eV (above 90 % confidence) — Phys. Rev. D 114, 103025, [10.1103/dgjv-chmt](https://doi.org/10.1103/dgjv-chmt)
- **2026-11-05 · xfel** — A simulation study: a plasma-photocathode wakefield accelerator could drive a high-gain X-ray laser in the water window (mJ, GW-class pulses) from a plasma stage about a metre long; not an experiment — Berman L. et al., Nature Communications, [10.1038/s41467-026-78880-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-78880-4) — [idw](https://idw-online.de/en/news880347)
- **2026-11-02 · particle** — **ALPS II** at DESY in Hamburg, a "light shining through a wall" experiment, first science run (February–May 2024): no signal; for pseudoscalar particles below ~0.1 meV the two-photon coupling is limited to g < 1.5 × 10⁻⁹ GeV⁻¹ (95 % CL), more than 20 times better than earlier experiments of this type. This is still far from the design goal of 2 × 10⁻¹¹ GeV⁻¹, and the optics are being upgraded — Brotherton D. et al., PRL, [10.1103/61sw-k3bc](https://doi.org/10.1103/61sw-k3bc); preprint [arXiv:2512.14110](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14110)
