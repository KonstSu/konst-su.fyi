## The 2026 Mars window (November to December)
- **MMX** (JAXA) launched on 19 October 2026 on an H3-24L: [mission page](mission:mmx).
- **ESCAPADE** (NASA) launched back in November 2025 and uses this window through its Earth flybys on 7 and 9 November: [mission page](mission:escapade).
- **Starship** (SpaceX): in 2025 Elon Musk put the chance of an uncrewed flight in the 2026 window at 50/50 ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/5/30/musk-says-50-50-chance-of-sending-uncrewed-starship-to-mars-by-late-2026)). A Mars Starship is not in the launch schedules, and SpaceX appears to have shifted its focus to the Moon. There has been no official cancellation; it is assumed here that it will not fly in 2026.

No other interplanetary launches are scheduled before the end of 2026.
