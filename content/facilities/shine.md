## Summary
A superconducting X-ray free-electron laser under construction since April 2018, the third continuous-wave superconducting XFEL after European XFEL and LCLS-II. The design: an 8 GeV linac, up to 1 MHz, and first-phase undulator lines covering 0.2–15 keV with femtosecond pulses.

## Now
**First lasing has been achieved.** On 21 October 2026 Xinhua, citing ShanghaiTech University, reported that SHINE had "successfully achieved free-electron laser first lasing", "a key step towards completion and use by the end of 2027". The linac currently runs as a 4 GeV continuous-wave superconducting machine (8 GeV after completion), with 18 undulators of 4 m and a 55 mm period ([Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/tech/20261021/6fd43feca5174cafb283b417e49c4744/c.html)). The report gives **neither the exact date, nor the undulator line, nor the photon energy**. The commissioning plan presented at IPAC'26 aimed at first lasing around 200 eV on the FEL-II line at about 4 GeV ([JACoW IPAC'26](https://indico.jacow.org/event/95/contributions/13077)), so the soft X-ray line is the likely candidate; this is not confirmed.
