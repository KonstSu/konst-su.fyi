## Summary
A superconducting X-ray free-electron laser under construction since 2018, the third in the world after European XFEL and LCLS-II, with three undulator lines and a photon range of 0.4–25 keV.

## Now
According to an IPAC'26 contribution, commissioning starts in July 2026 after the cryomodules are cooled down: first the accelerator and the FEL-II line at about 4 GeV, aiming at first lasing around 200 eV ([JACoW IPAC'26](https://indico.jacow.org/event/95/contributions/13077)). The project is due to be completed by the end of 2027 ([Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/video/103262190.html)). **First lasing has not yet been confirmed** in the sources found.
