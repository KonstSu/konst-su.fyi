## Summary
Two detectors under construction: ARCA (astrophysics, high energies) and ORCA (oscillations, neutrino mass ordering). They take data as they grow; the partly built ARCA has already recorded a record event.

## Now
The main topic is the event **KM3-230213A** (13 February 2023, estimated energy ~220 PeV), the most energetic neutrino ever recorded, published in Nature in February 2025. In 2026 the collaboration showed in JCAP that a population of blazars could explain the event without conflicting with gamma-ray and neutrino limits; other papers discuss a possible Galactic origin ([KM3NeT/ANTARES](https://antares.in2p3.fr/km3net-detects-the-highest-energy-neutrino-ever), [JCAP, PDF](https://cris.unibo.it/retrieve/32e7a5c0-d8fc-4562-8ae4-b88480141133/JCAP_KM3N3T_BlazarOriginUHEevent_26.pdf)). No second ultra-high-energy event has been reported so far.
