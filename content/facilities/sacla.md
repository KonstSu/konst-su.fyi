## Summary
The world's second hard X-ray free-electron laser after LCLS: first lasing on 7 June 2011 ([RIKEN history](https://xfel.riken.jp/eng/project/index.html)). It is compact thanks to its 5.7 GHz C-band linear accelerator ([RIKEN](https://xfel.riken.jp/eng/sacla/index02.html)). Construction started in April 2006 and general user operation in March 2012; it has three beamlines (SPring-8 on the same site has 57) ([MEXT, 29 September 2026](https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20260929-mxt_kibanken01-000052235_2.pdf)).

## Now
In user operation. The main event on the site is the SPring-8 upgrade (see [SPring-8](facility:spring-8)): according to MEXT, SPring-8 will stop for about a year from the second half of Japanese fiscal year 2027, and general use of SPring-8-II will begin during fiscal year 2029. The government's "Integrated Innovation Strategy 2026" asks for enough operating time at SPring-8/SACLA and J-PARC during that period so that research can continue ([MEXT, 29 September 2026](https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20260929-mxt_kibanken01-000052235_2.pdf)).
