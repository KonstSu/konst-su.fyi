## Summary
A $815 million upgrade: a new fourth-generation ring in the old tunnel, up to 500 times brighter than before, and nine new beamlines. Argonne calls the upgraded APS the brightest synchrotron in the world ([lightsources.org / Argonne, 5 February 2026](https://lightsources.org/2026/02/05/argonne-celebrates-successful-completion-of-the-aps-upgrade/)). Together with ESRF-EBS, it is the model for high-energy upgrades (PETRA IV, SPring-8-II).

## Now
The project is officially complete: on 5 February 2026 the US Department of Energy gave its final approval; scientists have been working at the upgraded facility for more than a year ([lightsources.org](https://lightsources.org/2026/02/05/argonne-celebrates-successful-completion-of-the-aps-upgrade/)). Beamlines are gradually returning to science, and some are still being commissioned ([APS Upgrade](https://www.aps.anl.gov/APS-Upgrade)).
