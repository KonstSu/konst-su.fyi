## Summary
The largest collider. The High-Luminosity LHC will increase the integrated luminosity about tenfold, with new Nb₃Sn quadrupoles, crab cavities and upgraded ATLAS and CMS detectors.

## Now
Run 3 is over, and **Long Shutdown 3 began at the LHC on 29 June 2026**. The LHC stops for 47 months; the injectors ran until September 2026. HL-LHC (Run 4) is due to start in June 2030 ([CERN](https://home.cern/updated-schedule-cerns-accelerators), [CERN ATS News](https://ats-news.web.cern.ch/?p=2009)). Civil engineering in the tunnel includes 28 vertical cores (1 m wide, 8 m high) that link the LHC tunnel with the new HL-LHC galleries ([CERN Courier, 13 November 2026](https://cerncourier.com/?p=117843)).

**Beyond the LHC.** On 22 May 2026 the CERN Council updated the European Strategy for Particle Physics and named **FCC-ee** the preferred next flagship collider. Construction is not approved: CERN management is to work out a funding plan with member and non-member states and the EU, so that the Council can decide by 2028. The first phase is estimated to cost about €16 billion ([FCC](https://fcc.web.cern.ch/cern-council-sets-course-future-particle-physics), [heise](https://www.heise.de/en/news/CERN-Council-Decides-on-Strategy-Update-FCC-ee-to-Become-LHC-Successor-11305189.html)).
