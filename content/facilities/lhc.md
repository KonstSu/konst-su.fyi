## Summary
The largest collider. The High-Luminosity LHC will increase the integrated luminosity about tenfold, with new Nb₃Sn quadrupoles, crab cavities and upgraded ATLAS and CMS detectors.

## Now
Run 3 is over, and **Long Shutdown 3 began at the LHC on 29 June 2026**. The LHC stops for 47 months; the injectors ran until September 2026. HL-LHC (Run 4) is due to start in June 2030 ([CERN](https://home.cern/updated-schedule-cerns-accelerators), [CERN ATS News](https://ats-news.web.cern.ch/?p=2009)).
