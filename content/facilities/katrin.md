## Summary
A 70-metre experiment with a MAC-E electrostatic spectrometer. It measures the endpoint of the tritium beta spectrum and from it the mass of the electron antineutrino, independently of cosmology.

## Now
Data taking **ended in October 2025**: 1,000 days of measurements in 19 campaigns, more than six times the statistics of the previous data set. The final analysis is under way ([KIT Indico, April 2026](https://indico.kit.edu/event/5330/contributions/22399/)). The current result is **m(ν) < 0.45 eV (90 % CL)** from the first five campaigns, published in Science on 10 April 2025 ([KATRIN](https://www.katrin.kit.edu/1271.php)).
