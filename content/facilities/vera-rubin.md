## Summary
The ten-year Legacy Survey of Space and Time: the whole southern sky every few nights in six filters. It has the world's largest digital camera (built at SLAC) and sends millions of alerts per night.

## Now
**The LSST survey officially began on the night of 29 June 2026** ([Princeton LSST](https://lsst.princeton.edu/news/2026/legacy-survey-space-and-time-starts-its-ten-year-survey), [Fermilab](https://astro.fnal.gov/?p=28612)). The first public alerts went out on 24 February 2026 ([SEA](https://www.sea-astronomia.es/boletin/el-observatorio-vera-c-rubin-y-el-legacy-survey-space-and-time-lsst)).
