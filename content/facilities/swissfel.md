## Summary
PSI's compact X-ray free-electron laser, in operation since 2016: electron energy 2.1–5.8 GeV, 100 pulses per second on each line. Aramis covers 1–7 Å and Athos 6.5–50 Å, together 0.25–12 keV; there are five experimental stations, with a sixth under construction ([SwissFEL](https://www.psi.ch/en/swissfel), [accelerator](https://www.psi.ch/en/swissfel/accelerator)). With a free-electron laser and a detector group on the same site, SwissFEL is a useful comparison to the megahertz European XFEL: a different pulse structure sets different requirements for the detectors.

## Now
The user programme runs as normal. The next step is **SwissFELplus**: according to T. Schietinger's talk at IPAC'26 (21 May 2026), the project is planned for 2029–2032 and should bring full coherence in hard and soft X-rays through self-seeding and seeding with an external laser, and prepare a new line ([JACoW IPAC'26, THP2084](https://indico.jacow.org/event/95/contributions/14458/)). Earlier, a third line, Porthos (very hard X-rays), appeared in the PSI roadmap for 2029/30 ([PSI slides, 2021](https://indico.psi.ch/event/10704/contributions/27137/attachments/18448/29027/20210319_Porthos_Update.pdf)); how Porthos relates to the "new line" of SwissFELplus is not yet clear. The long shutdown of the HIPA proton complex (SINQ, SμS, CHRISP; from late 2027 to September 2029) does not affect SwissFEL.
