## Summary
The UK's national synchrotron. **Diamond-II**: energy from 3.0 to 3.5 GeV, natural emittance from 2,729 to 162 pm·rad, with a modified hybrid six-bend achromat lattice (M-H6BA); the project was approved in July 2023 ([R. Fielder, Diamond Update, ESLS 2025](https://indico.synchrotron-soleil.fr/event/140/contributions/725/attachments/379/578/ESLS2025_Diamond.pdf)).

## Now
According to Diamond's slides (ESLS, 30 October 2025): the dark period begins in December 2027; commissioning of the ring with beam follows in December 2028; the dark period ends in June 2029; the first phase of beamlines is in operation in September 2029; and the project is completed in March 2030. So far, delays are absorbed by contingency in the schedule, with readiness reviews every six months since December 2025 ([ESLS 2025](https://indico.synchrotron-soleil.fr/event/140/contributions/725/attachments/379/578/ESLS2025_Diamond.pdf)). The Diamond Extension Building opened in 2026 ([Diamond News](https://www.diamond.ac.uk/Home/News/LatestNews/2026/Diamond-marks-the-opening-of-the-Diamond-Extension-Building.html)). The schedule here is taken from those conference slides; see also the [Diamond-II page](https://www.diamond.ac.uk/Diamond-II.html).
