## Summary
A high-energy fourth-generation light source in Beijing, according to Chinese sources the first of its kind in Asia. The 6 GeV ring has a circumference of about 1.36 km (design parameters, [IHEP HEPS](https://english.ihep.cas.cn/heps/)). The first phase has 14 user beamlines and one test beamline, with X-rays up to 300 keV; the building has room for at least 90 beamlines ([Science and Technology Daily, 15 June 2026](http://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-06/15/content_532140.html)).

## Now
**In trial operation; national acceptance has not yet taken place.** Acceptance of the parameters by the Chinese Academy of Sciences came on 29 October 2025; trial user experiments began in December 2025, and by 11 February 2026, 222 projects had been carried out on 13 beamlines. IHEP planned the official start for mid-2026; the International Advisory Committee (25–27 March 2026) named the move to stable operation and the preparation of the second phase as the main tasks ([IHEP, 31 March 2026](https://english.ihep.cas.cn/nw/han/y26/202603/t20260331_1154315.html)). The first general call for proposals (March to 30 April 2026) drew 1,740 proposals in 40 days; by the end of April there were about 300 projects and more than 1,000 users. In June 2026 the management spoke of national acceptance and an official start "within the year" ([Science and Technology Daily, 15 June 2026](http://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-06/15/content_532140.html)). As of November 2026, no report of the acceptance has been found.
