## Summary
**PETRA III**: 6 GeV, 100 mA, a circumference of 2,304 m, horizontal emittance 1.2–1.3 nm·rad (two DESY pages give different values), 25 user beamlines ([PETRA III](https://photon-science.desy.de/facilities/petra_iii/index_eng.html), [parameters](https://photon-science.desy.de/facilities/petra_iii/machine/parameters/index_eng.html)). **PETRA IV** would rebuild the ring in the existing tunnel as a fourth-generation source: the beam would be focused 100 to 1,000 times better, with 36 beamlines in four halls ([PETRA IV](https://petra4.desy.de/petra_iv/index_eng.html)). The cost is about €1.8 billion (2025 prices) over nine years. Together with European XFEL and FLASH, it makes Hamburg the largest centre of X-ray sources; for detector developers, PETRA IV would be another major customer.

## Now
**There is no funding decision yet.** On 27 March 2026 the German Science Council (Wissenschaftsrat) rated PETRA IV as scientifically highly relevant, internationally outstanding and strategically important; the final decision lies with the federal research ministry (BMFTR). DESY estimates that with a quick positive decision the project could start as early as 2027, with X-rays for experiments around 2032 ([DESY Photon Science, 27 March 2026](https://photon-science.desy.de/news__events/news__highlights/desy's_future_project_petra_iv_wins_over_the_german_science_council/index_eng.html)). DESY had said it was important to have a decision by mid-2026 at the latest ([DESY News, 2025](https://desy.de/desy_latest_news/2025/petra_iv/index_eng.html)); as of November 2026, no decision has been reported. Under the current plan, construction of the experimental hall in Lise-Meitner-Park would start in 2028 and take about two years; it cannot start before the project is approved ([PETRA IV — construction](https://petra4.desy.de/standort/bauvorhaben/index_ger.html)).

**No date has been announced for shutting down PETRA III.** Documents from 2022–2023 mentioned the end of 2026 or January 2027; those dates are out of date.
