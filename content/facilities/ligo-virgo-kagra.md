## Summary
A network of interferometers with 3–4 km arms. Since 2015 it has recorded hundreds of mergers of black holes and neutron stars. The next big step is the O5 run with the A+ upgrade.

## Now
The O4 run ended on 18 November 2025, and the detectors are being upgraded and recommissioned. **The start of the IR1 run appears to have slipped.** The official LVK plan (last updated 17 April 2026) gave a six-month IR1 run starting between late October and mid-November 2026 ([LVK observing plans](https://observing.docs.ligo.org/plan/index.html)). The AEI page now says that the collaborations are "targeting a mid-January 2027 start", "potentially starting with two of the three detectors" (LIGO and Virgo), with KAGRA joining later ([AEI](https://www.aei.mpg.de/gwastronomy)). No announcement that the run has started was found. The O5 schedule is being reassessed.

New results from O4: GWTC-5.0 (the second part of O4, data up to 28 January 2025) brings the catalogue to more than 300 merging binaries — preprint [arXiv:2605.27223](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27223); a paper in Physical Review D constrains ultralight bosons using remnant black holes from O4b and O4c (see Results).

## Results
- 2026-11-12 — **Ultralight bosons from merging and remnant black holes (O4b, O4c):** searches for long transients from vector-boson clouds around merger remnants disfavour vector masses of ≈2.80–3.95 × 10⁻¹³ eV (above 90 % confidence) — LVK, Phys. Rev. D 114, 103025, [10.1103/dgjv-chmt](https://doi.org/10.1103/dgjv-chmt)
- 2026-09-22 — **Direct search for several kinds of dark matter (O4a):** scalar (dilaton), dark-photon and tensor ultralight dark matter; no signal, with limits orders of magnitude better than earlier experiments — LVK, Phys. Rev. Lett. 137, 131001, [10.1103/qdnd-rsnh](https://doi.org/10.1103/qdnd-rsnh) — [LIGO summary](https://ligo.org/science-summaries/o4a_dark_matter/)
- 2026-05-26 — **GWTC-5.0**: the second part of O4 (to 28 January 2025), more than 300 merging binaries in total — preprint [arXiv:2605.27223](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27223)
- 2025/2026 — **GWTC-4.0** (first part of O4, O4a): 128 significant events, about 218 in the catalogue in total — [LVK](https://ligo.org/ligo-virgo-kagra-collaboration-successfully-wraps-up-its-fourth-observing-run/)
