## Summary
A network of interferometers with 3–4 km arms. Since 2015 it has recorded hundreds of mergers of black holes and neutron stars. The next big step is the O5 run with the A+ upgrade.

## Now
The O4 run ended on 18 November 2025, and the detectors are being upgraded and recommissioned. The official plan is a six-month **IR1** run starting between late October and mid-November 2026, with both LIGO detectors, Virgo with interruptions and KAGRA when ready. The O5 schedule is being revised ([LVK observing plans](https://observing.docs.ligo.org/plan/index.html)). As of 4 November 2026 I found no confirmation that IR1 had started.
