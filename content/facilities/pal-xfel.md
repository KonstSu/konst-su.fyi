## Summary
Korea's X-ray free-electron laser: 10 GeV electrons, an undulator of about 100 m, 60 Hz. Hard X-rays 0.06–1 nm (2.2–15 keV at the beamline), soft X-rays 1–10 nm (250–1,200 eV) ([PAL](https://pal.postech.ac.kr/en/pal/deviceIntrcn.do), [beamlines](https://pal.postech.ac.kr/en/intro/plsbeamLineMapXFEL.do)). The first phase built HX1 and SX1; the design allows for three hard (HX1–HX3) and two soft (SX1–SX2) lines. Stations: XSS (FXS, FXL) and NCI (CXS, SFX) on the hard X-ray line; XAS/XES (since 2017), RSXS (since 2020) and FTH (since 2021) on the soft X-ray line.

## Now
According to the plenary talk at LINAC2026 (Daejeon, 17 August 2026), the machine runs stably at 10.5 GeV, and **funding for the second line, HX2, started in 2026**: 1.5–10 keV, more than 3 mJ between 1.5 and 8 keV (twice HX1), 20 undulators with self-seeding, and two new stations. The hard and soft X-ray lines will run simultaneously at 60 Hz each; a resonant kicker (about 50 MHz, as at SwissFEL) separates two bunches about 25 ns apart ([LINAC2026, MO1A001, slides](https://indico.jacow.org/event/117/contributions/15304/attachments/2509/8977/MO1A001_talk.pdf)). A PAL poster at the same conference gives HX2 as 2–10 keV at 180–200 pC and 30 Hz ([MC2.A04](https://indico.jacow.org/event/117/contributions/15348/)), so the parameters are still being refined. The IPAC'23 plan put HX2 construction at about two years and about USD 100 million ([IPAC'23 PAPU](https://ipac23org.elettra.eu/books/PAPU/75/)); no official start date has been given.
