## Summary
A planned European underground interferometer of the third generation (arms of about 10 km or more, cryogenics, sensitivity at low frequencies). It will observe mergers across the whole observable universe.

## Now
There are three candidate sites. In July 2026 Lusatia and Sardinia announced a **joint bid** ([TU Dresden](https://tu-dresden.de/kooperation/news/einstein-telescope-lausitz-und-sardinien-besiegeln-gemeinsame-bewerbung?set_language=en)). Bid books are due by the end of 2026 ([Physics World](https://physicsworld.com/a/physicists-set-to-decide-location-for-next-generation-einstein-telescope/)). A presentation from January 2026 puts the official site selection in the second quarter of 2027 ([Nikhef, PDF](https://indico.nikhef.nl/event/7509/contributions/28333/attachments/10479/15977/20260113-noise.pdf)), while reports from July say "end of 2027". Earlier expectations of a decision in 2026 are out of date.
