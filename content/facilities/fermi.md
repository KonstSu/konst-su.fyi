## Summary
The first externally seeded free-electron laser open to users: FEL-1 covers 100–20 nm (single-stage harmonic cascade), FEL-2 20–4 nm (two-stage cascade). The linear accelerator was designed for about 1.5 GeV and reached 1,663 MeV in May 2025 ([description](https://www.elettra.eu/lightsources/fermi/fermi-machine/fermi-description.html), [milestones](https://www.elettra.eu/lightsources/fermi/fermi-machine/milestones.html)). First lasing was in December 2010; FEL-2 opened to users in June 2016. Beamlines: DiProI, EIS-TIMEX, EIS-TIMER, LDM, MAGNEDYN and TeraFERMI.

## Now
The first phase of the FERMI 2.0 upgrade is complete: FEL-1 now uses the EEHG seeding scheme, and the linac energy has been raised to 1.5 GeV at 50 Hz (above 1.65 GeV at 10 Hz). FERMI 2.0 aims to extend the range into the "water window" and beyond, and to shorten the pulses to the core-hole lifetime of light elements ([FEL2024, L. Giannessi](https://indico.jacow.org/event/72/contributions/5520/)). No schedule for the next phases has been published. The neighbouring Elettra storage ring has been shut down for its upgrade since July 2025 (see [Elettra 2.0](facility:elettra)); the sources found do not confirm whether FERMI has been running for users throughout.
