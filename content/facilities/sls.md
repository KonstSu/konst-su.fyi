## Summary
The SLS upgrade began in September 2023: a new ring based on a seven-bend achromat with reverse bends, energy raised from 2.4 to 2.7 GeV, emittance improved up to 40 times, and brightness up to 1,000 times higher on some beamlines; two superconducting 5 T superbends, and NEG-coated vacuum chambers ([SLS 2.0](https://www.psi.ch/en/sls2-0), [accelerator](https://www.psi.ch/en/sls2-0/accelerator)). PSI's detector group, which develops hybrid pixel detectors, works at SLS.

## Now
Electrons have been back in the ring since February 2025 (PSI news of 25 February 2025), and the new SLS opened on 21 August 2025. PSI's plan: pilot users from the second half of 2025; a short second shutdown in early 2026 (additional undulators and superbends); regular operation of the first phase of beamlines from mid-2026; the second phase of beamlines from mid-2026; most beamlines by autumn 2026 ([SLS 2.0](https://www.psi.ch/en/sls2-0), [schedule](https://www.psi.ch/en/sls2-0/time-schedule), "subject to change"). Whether the plan has been met as of November 2026 has not been checked.
