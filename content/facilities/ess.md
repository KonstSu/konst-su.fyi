## Summary
The world's most powerful pulsed neutron source: 2 GeV protons, a rotating tungsten target and 15 instruments in the final configuration. A close technological relative of European XFEL through its superconducting linac.

## Now
The accelerator has already delivered full-energy beam to a tuning dump. First beam on target has **moved from 2026 to 27 January 2027** (ESS update of April 2026); the date depends on the delivery of the MRP2 target component. In late 2025 a voltage surge damaged the moderator and delayed the work. Four instruments are ready to receive neutrons ([ESS STAP, April 2026, PDF](https://indico.ess.eu/event/4007/contributions/24588/attachments/17817/35512/intro-STAPs-apr-2026.pdf)).
