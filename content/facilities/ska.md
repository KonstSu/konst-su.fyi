## Summary
The largest radio-astronomy observatory under construction: the epoch of reionisation, pulsars (including pulsar timing for gravitational waves) and cosmic magnetism.

## Now
SKA-Low released its first image in March 2025 (the first four stations, 1,024 antennas) — [Physics World](https://physicsworld.com/a/milestone-as-square-kilometre-array-observatory-releases-its-first-low-frequency-image-of-the-cosmos/). SKA-Mid obtained its **first interference fringes** in January 2026 with two dishes; the first image from a four-dish array was expected "in the coming months" and has not been confirmed yet ([Creamer Media](https://newsletter.en.creamermedia.com/article/the-ska-mid-telescope-array-has-passed-its-first-true-test-as-a-science-instrument-2026-01-07)).
