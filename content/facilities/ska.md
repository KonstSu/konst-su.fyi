## Summary
The largest radio-astronomy observatory under construction: the epoch of reionisation, pulsars (including pulsar timing for gravitational waves) and cosmic magnetism.

## Now
SKA-Low released its first image in March 2025 (the first four stations, 1,024 antennas) — [Physics World](https://physicsworld.com/a/milestone-as-square-kilometre-array-observatory-releases-its-first-low-frequency-image-of-the-cosmos/). SKA-Mid obtained its **first interference fringes** in January 2026 with two dishes; the first image from a four-dish array was expected "within the next few months", but as of 23 November 2026 the SKAO news page has not confirmed it ([SKAO](https://www.skao.int/en/news/693/ska-mid-milestone)).

On 1 June 2026 France became the 14th member state, and Prof. Jessica Dempsey took office as Director-General ([SKAO news](https://www.skao.int/en/news)).
