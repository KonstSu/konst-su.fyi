## Summary
The first facility to use a multi-bend achromat (MBA) in a large ring. It opened in 2016 and set the direction for every fourth-generation light source. The 3 GeV ring: a circumference of 528 m, horizontal emittance of about 200–330 pm·rad depending on the undulator gaps, 10 beamlines ([3 GeV ring](https://www.maxiv.lu.se/beamlines-accelerators/accelerators/3-gev-storage-ring/)). The 1.5 GeV ring: 96 m, 6 nm·rad, a compact double-bend achromat ([1.5 GeV ring](https://www.maxiv.lu.se/beamlines-accelerators/accelerators/1-5-gev-storage-ring/)).

## Now
In user operation. The **SXL** project (Soft X-ray Laser) would add a soft X-ray free-electron laser on the existing 3 GeV linac: a new 40-metre undulator, a beamline and stations. The conceptual design (funded by the Knut and Alice Wallenberg Foundation and MAX IV) is complete and serves as the basis for discussions with users; the website gives no schedule or budget ([SXL](https://www.maxiv.lu.se/beamlines-accelerators/accelerators/soft-x-ray-laser/)). The Swedish user organisation included SXL among MAX IV's prospects in its report to the Swedish Research Council ([VR, 2025](https://www.vr.se/download/18.39275e119a0f6d9df21fe24/1762271218121/Swedish-needs-in-X-ray-science-2025.pdf)).
