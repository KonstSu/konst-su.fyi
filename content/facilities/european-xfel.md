## Summary
The world's largest X-ray free-electron laser: a 1.7 km superconducting linear accelerator at 17.5 GeV, trains of up to 2,700 pulses at an intra-train rate of 4.5 MHz, three SASE beamlines and seven instruments. This megahertz pulse structure is what made dedicated detectors necessary (AGIPD, LPD, DSSC, JUNGFRAU).

## Now
Back in user operation after its first long shutdown with a warm-up of the accelerator (LIMP, 2025). The restart was celebrated on 11 February 2026, and the user programme resumed in spring 2026 ([idw](https://nachrichten.idw-online.de/2026/02/11/european-xfel-celebrates-successful-restart)). A new photoinjector, GUN5, was installed during the shutdown ([DESY](https://desy.de/desy_latest_news/2025/xfelmaintenance/index_eng.html), [lightsources.org](https://lightsources.org/2026/02/11/european-xfel-celebrates-a-successful-restart/)).

Two records were published this autumn:
- **30 keV (≈0.04 nm) X-ray laser pulses**, delivered reliably at 800 pulses per second and transported along the ~1 km beamline with almost 60 % transmission — published in Optica, press release of 20 November 2026. The previous record at the facility (2020) was 25 keV lasing on SASE1 at 17.5 GeV ([lightsources.org](https://lightsources.org/2026/11/20/european-xfel-takes-x-ray-laser-to-extremely-short-wavelengths/), [European XFEL](https://www.xfel.eu/news_and_events/news/index_eng.html?openDirectAnchor=3119)).
- **Attosecond hard X-ray pulses above 10²⁰ W/cm²**: pulses of about 300 as focused to 180 × 330 nm with an advanced Kirkpatrick–Baez (AKB) mirror system — Inoue I. et al., Optica, [10.1364/optica.613284](https://doi.org/10.1364/optica.613284) ([idw, 16 October 2026](https://idw-online.de/en/news879281)).

Preparations for a facility upgrade are under way; the public [upgrade page](https://www.xfel.eu/facility/upgrade/index_eng.html) describes the options being studied, including changes to the accelerator, new FEL sources and instruments.

## Results
- 2026-11-20 — **30 keV (≈0.04 nm) X-ray laser pulses for users**: 800 pulses per second, ~60 % transmission over ~1 km; this extends the photon-energy range for experiments on dense matter and at extreme pressures — Optica (DOI not yet indexed) — [lightsources.org](https://lightsources.org/2026/11/20/european-xfel-takes-x-ray-laser-to-extremely-short-wavelengths/)
- 2026-09-23 — **Record-intensity attosecond hard X-ray pulses** (above 10²⁰ W/cm², ~300 as) — Inoue I. et al., *Generation of high-fluence and high-intensity hard x-ray attosecond pulses at European XFEL*, Optica, [10.1364/optica.613284](https://doi.org/10.1364/optica.613284) — [idw](https://idw-online.de/en/news879281)
- 2026-01-28 — **XFELO:** first demonstration of clean amplification of hard X-rays in a cavity (~66 m, diamond mirrors), a step towards an X-ray laser oscillator with a very narrow spectrum — [CFEL](https://www.cfel.de/news/novel_xfelo_laser/index_eng.html); paper: Nature, [10.1038/s41586-025-10025-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-10025-x)
