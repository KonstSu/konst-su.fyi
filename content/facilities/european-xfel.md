## Summary
The world's largest X-ray free-electron laser: a 1.7 km superconducting linear accelerator at 17.5 GeV, trains of up to 2,700 pulses at an intra-train rate of 4.5 MHz, three SASE beamlines and seven instruments. This megahertz pulse structure is what made dedicated detectors necessary (AGIPD, LPD, DSSC, JUNGFRAU).

## Now
Back in user operation after its first long shutdown with a warm-up of the accelerator (LIMP, 2025). The restart was celebrated on 11 February 2026, and the user programme resumed in spring 2026 ([idw](https://nachrichten.idw-online.de/2026/02/11/european-xfel-celebrates-successful-restart)). A new photoinjector, GUN5, was installed during the shutdown ([DESY](https://desy.de/desy_latest_news/2025/xfelmaintenance/index_eng.html), [lightsources.org](https://lightsources.org/2026/02/11/european-xfel-celebrates-a-successful-restart/)). Preparations for a facility upgrade are under way; the public [upgrade page](https://www.xfel.eu/facility/upgrade/index_eng.html) describes the options being studied.
