## Summary
SPring-8 is an 8 GeV ring with 57 beamlines, in general user operation since October 1997; the X-ray laser [SACLA](facility:sacla) is on the same site. **SPring-8-II** is an upgrade to world-leading level: about 100 times the present brightness, with a five-bend lattice; the work takes five years, including a shutdown of about one year ([MEXT, 29 September 2026](https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20260929-mxt_kibanken01-000052235_2.pdf)). The first new SPring-8-II beamline is BL34XU for hydrogen energy research (fuel cells and electrolysis under operating conditions).

## Now
**The operator has named the month of the shutdown.** On 29 September 2026 JASRI told the MEXT quantum-beam committee that SPring-8 will stop for about one year **from August 2027** ([JASRI, MEXT committee document 3](https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20260929-mxt_kibanken01-000052235_3.pdf)). The MEXT document of the same day is less precise: a shutdown of about one year from the second half of Japanese fiscal year 2027, and general use of SPring-8-II **during fiscal year 2029** (April 2029 to March 2030). The request for fiscal year 2027 is ¥9.0 billion for SPring-8-II (on top of ¥15.4 billion in the fiscal year 2025 supplementary budget), plus measures for the shutdown period: a single access point to other quantum-beam facilities, and enough operating time at SPring-8/SACLA and J-PARC ([MEXT](https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20260929-mxt_kibanken01-000052235_2.pdf)). JASRI is arranging for instruments to move to other Japanese light sources (NanoTerasu, UVSOR and others) during the shutdown. On 16 November 2026 RIKEN presented a review of how SPring-8 and SACLA should operate in the SPring-8-II era ([RIKEN, MEXT committee document 5](https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20261116-mxt_kibanken01-000052869_5.pdf)).
