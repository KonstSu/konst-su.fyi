## Summary
SPring-8 is an 8 GeV ring with 57 beamlines, in general user operation since October 1997; the X-ray laser [SACLA](facility:sacla) is on the same site. **SPring-8-II** is an upgrade to world-leading level: about 100 times the present brightness, with a five-bend lattice; the work takes five years, including a shutdown of about one year ([MEXT, 29 September 2026](https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20260929-mxt_kibanken01-000052235_2.pdf)). The first new SPring-8-II beamline is BL34XU for hydrogen energy research (fuel cells and electrolysis under operating conditions).

## Now
According to the MEXT document of 29 September 2026, SPring-8-II will be built over five years, including a shutdown of about one year **from the second half of Japanese fiscal year 2027** (so not before October 2027), and general use of SPring-8-II will begin **during fiscal year 2029** (April 2029 to March 2030). The request for fiscal year 2027 is ¥9 billion for SPring-8-II and ¥984 million for measures during the shutdown: a single access point to other quantum-beam facilities, and keeping operating time at SPring-8/SACLA and J-PARC ([MEXT](https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20260929-mxt_kibanken01-000052235_2.pdf)). **The month of the shutdown has not been specified:** some secondary sources say "from August 2027", which could not be confirmed in the primary source.
