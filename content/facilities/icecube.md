## Summary
A cubic kilometre of Antarctic ice instrumented with about 5,000 optical modules. It discovered high-energy astrophysical neutrinos (2013) and the first sources (TXS 0506+056, NGC 1068, the Galactic plane).

## Now
**The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics went to Francis Halzen** "for his decisive contributions to the creation of the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin" (announced 6 October 2026) — [Nobel Prize](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2026/summary/), [Physics World](https://physicsworld.com/a/francis-halzen-wins-nobel-prize-in-physics/). **IceCube Upgrade** was deployed in the 2025/26 season: construction finished in January 2026 and first data are coming in ([UW News](https://news.wisc.edu/icecube-neutrino-observatory-gets-a-major-upgrade-beneath-the-ice/), [IceCube-Gen2, May 2026](https://indico.global/event/17941/contributions/154033/attachments/70322/136500/ICGen2_May2026.pdf)). Sources differ on the number of new strings (six or seven).
