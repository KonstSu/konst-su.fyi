## Summary
The successor to Super-Kamiokande, about eight times larger in volume. Its goals are CP violation in neutrinos (with the J-PARC beam), proton decay and neutrinos from supernovae. A direct competitor of [DUNE](facility:dune).

## Now
Excavation of the cavern was completed in summer 2025; the tank is being built in 2026, photomultipliers will be installed in 2027, and first data are expected in 2028 ([PoS, Hyper-K status](https://pos.sissa.it/516/010/), [CERN Indico, January 2026](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1568942/contributions/6841210)).
