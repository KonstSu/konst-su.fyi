## Summary
France's 2.75 GeV synchrotron. **SOLEIL II** brings a new ultra-low-emittance ring and a new booster at the same energy: natural emittance from 3,900 to 90 pm·rad ([L. Nadolski, ESLS 2025](https://indico.synchrotron-soleil.fr/event/140/contributions/723/attachments/389/597/2025-10_ESLS_SOLEIL_nadolski_v2.pdf)); it extends the facility's life by at least 15 years ([SOLEIL II](https://www.synchrotron-soleil.fr/en/soleil-ii-building-tomorrows-synchrotron-today)).

## Now
Preparing for the shutdown. The RF roadmap: conditioning from August to December 2026; the first two 352 MHz cavities installed in the present ring in July–August 2027; the first 1.4 GHz harmonic cavity in January 2028; **SOLEIL shuts down in October 2028** ([ESLS 2025](https://indico.synchrotron-soleil.fr/event/140/contributions/723/attachments/389/597/2025-10_ESLS_SOLEIL_nadolski_v2.pdf)). The dark period starts in October 2028 and lasts about two years including commissioning ([DEELS 2026, N. Hubert](https://indico.psi.ch/event/17840/contributions/62362/)). Users would return around the end of 2030; this is an estimate from those dates, and no official date has been found.
