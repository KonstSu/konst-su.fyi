## Summary
The world's first hard X-ray free-electron laser (2009). LCLS-II added a 4 GeV superconducting accelerator running continuously; the LCLS-II-HE upgrade raises the energy to 8 GeV (184 new superconducting cavities with an average gradient of 22 MV/m) and extends the hard X-ray range at high repetition rate.

## Now
The superconducting linac has been off since late 2025 for the LCLS-II-HE installation; the accelerator is expected to restart in early 2027, while the copper linac keeps running ([JACoW IPAC/LINAC](https://indico.jacow.org/event/89/contributions/8869/)). The LCLS town hall plan (July 2025) foresaw a long shutdown from January 2026 to January 2027 and first HE light in October 2027 ([LCLS Town Hall, PDF](https://lcls.slac.stanford.edu/sites/default/files/2025-07/2025.07.16%20LCLS_TownHall_Combined.pdf)).
